Saiyaara movie review : Ahaan Panday, Aneet Padda's chemistry, soulful music give life to Mohit Suri's cliched love story

Director : Mohit Suri

Hero : Ahaan Panday
Heroine : Aneet Padda

Movie plot : Krish Kapur is an egoistic, short-tempered musician in search of a big break. He meets Vaani, a shy, aspiring journalist who was once left at the altar. Krish chances upon her diary, filled with lyrics. He turns them into songs—and they’re a hit. Love follows. But fate plays spoilsport, and the two are separated. Will they reunite, or are they destined to be star-crossed lovers?

Rating : 9.5/10
