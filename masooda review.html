Masooda review : Masooda is a 2022 Indian Telugu-language supernatural horror film written and directed by debutant Sai Kiran, and produced by Rahul Yadav Nakka through the Swadharm Entertainment. The film stars Sangeetha, Thiruveer, Kavya Kalyanram, and Subhalekha Sudhakar. The film was released on 18 November 2022, opened to positive reviews from critics and audiences, alike, and became a commercial success.

Director : Thiruveer

Hero : Niloy Alamgir
Heroine : Anjuman

Movie plot : In 1989, Chittoor, Fardeen clandestinely attempts to help his younger brother Faizal in leaving the village but a mysterious woman gives them a chase through sugarcane fields, detains them and takes them to a ruined bungalow in the forest. Witnessing this, the brothers' relative Nargis notifies the siblings' elder brother Mir of it and together, they head to the bungalow for rescuing them. They meet with an accident due to a falling tree amidst heavy rain, that renders an eye of Nargis to be damaged. Meanwhile, the woman performs Black magic on the brothers and takes assistance from another person to engage in human sacrifice.

Rating : 9/10
